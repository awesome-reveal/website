## Reveal from your state

- `when` in React, Vue and Astro tabs (the Details toggle examples). Astro sets `host.when`.

## Play the entrance again

- Set `when` to `false`, then to `true`.

## Hide the content

- `when` does not hide content. Use `hidden` or do not render it. `inert` does not hide.
- Reduced motion reveals the element also when `when` is `false`.

## Play clipped content by hand

- Core has no manual trigger. Play the paused animations through `getAnimations()`, with `{ subtree: true }` for a cascade.
