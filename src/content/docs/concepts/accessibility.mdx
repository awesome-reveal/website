## Reduced motion

- At call time, no animations. A change to reduce shows waiting content.

## Focus

- Focus in a waiting element shows it at once, with no entrance.

## Screen readers

- Waiting content stays in the accessibility tree. `when={false}` does not hide content.

## Print

- Waiting content shows in print. DevTools print emulation does not fire the print events.
