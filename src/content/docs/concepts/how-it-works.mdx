## A paused animation

- `element.animate()` with `fill: "backwards"`, paused at its start.

## The viewport check

- One observer per element, or per cascade container. Thresholds `[0, threshold]`.
- A start state with `opacity: 0` waits at the resting position.

## One entrance

- The observation stops at the reveal. No exit animation. Finished reveals release themselves.

## The framework packages

- Core holds the behavior. The packages bind it to the render lifecycle and add `when` and `onReveal`.

## Fallbacks

- No Intersection Observer: plays at once. No `Element.animate`: shows at once.
- Browser errors are reported, and the content shows.

## Things to know

- Do not reveal one element in two calls.
- Skip paused animations when you wait for `document.getAnimations()`.
