## How it works in Astro

- A custom element and a module script. No `client:*` directive and no stylesheet import.

## Where to place Reveal

- Not directly inside `<table>`, its sections and rows, or `<select>`.
- Inside a `<p>` or inline MDX text, use an inline `as`, such as `span`.

## View transitions

- `<ClientRouter />`, `astro:page-load` and `transition:persist`.

## Server islands

- `server:defer` content reveals.

## Content Security Policy

- `security.csp: true` (not with `<ClientRouter />`) or `build.inlineStylesheets: 'never'`.
